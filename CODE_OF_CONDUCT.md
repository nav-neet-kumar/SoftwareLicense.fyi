# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want IsItFree? to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for everyone. Contributors and community members should have a harassment-free experience regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on the issue, idea, or code rather than attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing other people's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention, doxxing, and abusive or threatening language are not acceptable. Spam, malicious submissions, and intentionally misleading software-license reports are also not welcome.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces when someone is representing or participating in IsItFree?.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub: https://github.com/nav-neet-kumar/SoftwareLicense.fyi. For sensitive reports, do not post personal or confidential details in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.
