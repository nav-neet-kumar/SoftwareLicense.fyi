Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how IsItFree? (“we,” “us,” or “the site”) handles information when you visit isitfree.webline.cloud.

Information and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage. Google may process pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, device/browser details, and interaction events, subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy. A public visit counter is provided by Hits.sh; loading it may send technical request information to that provider.

The site does not require an account to browse the directory. Search and filtering happen in your browser.

Suggestions and error reports

Suggestion and error-report forms prepare a GitHub issue title and body, then open GitHub's issue-creation page in a new tab. The site does not ask for, receive, or store a GitHub personal access token. You may need to sign in to GitHub and press Submit there; the report is not sent until you complete that step. If submitted, the issue and its contents may be public on GitHub. The prefilled details are passed to GitHub in the page URL, so do not include passwords, tokens, confidential information, or other sensitive personal data.

GitHub handles submitted issue data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. The site uses browser local storage to remember your light/dark theme preference. That preference stays in your browser and is not used by the site to identify you.

External websites

Software listings link to official vendor sites, GitHub, and other external websites. We do not control those sites or their privacy practices; review their policies before sharing information.

Retention and your choices

IsItFree? does not operate an account system or first-party user profile database. Analytics and public issue data may be retained by third-party providers under their own policies. You can limit cookies through browser settings; some analytics functionality may be affected.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. For questions or privacy-related requests, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.
